STEP 02 · TURNING TEXT INTO NUMBERS

Your sentence is divided into small pieces

A helper called a tokenizer breaks a sentence into small pieces. Each piece gets an ID number, like a library catalogue number that helps the computer find the right entry.

Remember: The ID is a lookup number, not the meaning of the word. A piece can be a whole word, part of a word, punctuation mark, or space. The IDs and count shown here are examples, not results from a live tokenizer.
